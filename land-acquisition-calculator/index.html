FAQ

Land acquisition compensation questions

How does the land acquisition calculator work out compensation under the RFCTLARR Act 2013?

For each survey number: market value (Sec 26) × the First Schedule multiplication factor, plus the value of assets attached to the land (Sec 29), plus 100 % solatium on that amount (Sec 30(1)), plus the 12 % a year additional amount on the market value for the actual period from the 3A / Sec 11 date to the award or possession, whichever is earlier (Sec 30(3)). Sec 28 heads and R&R under the Second Schedule are added on top. Government land is not compensated unless you switch it on.

How is the market value chosen under Sec 26?

By default it is the higher of three: the guidance (stamp-duty) value, the average of the top half of the sale deeds by price per acre over the three years before the 3A / Sec 11 date, and any consented amount. Deeds can be excluded with a reason (an earlier LA award, or not showing the real market), and duplicates are dropped. You can also choose guidance only or sale deeds only.

Can I import an LA schedule from Excel when my headings are different?

Yes. The import finds the header row, even below gazette text, and joins two-row headings. It matches your column names to the app's fields using synonyms and tolerates spelling slips; each match is shown as sure, guessed or not found, and you can change any of them. Units are read from the headings (sq m, ha, acre, gunta, '0 Acre 13 Gunta'). Before anything is added, a row check fills merged villages down, skips numbering, total and signature rows, and checks the total rows against the rows above. Your Excel file is only read, never changed.

What if village names are spelt differently in different files?

The import matches a name with its words swapped or a letter different, and each part of an 'X uruf Y' name, to the villages in the land schedule. Each match is listed as a CHECK and can be corrected under Other spellings on the Villages page. Names that differ only in a short part, such as Village A and Village B or East and West, are never merged.

Does it track the 3A, 3C and 3D lapse periods?

Yes. It shows the 21-day objection period after the 3A notification, keeps the 3C objections register, and warns that the 3A lapses if the 3D declaration is not published within one year. The 3D schedule is the 3A schedule less the land dropped or reduced on objections, with a 3A vs 3D comparison. On the RFCTLARR track it follows Sec 11, 15, 19 and 23.

Can the LA cost and schedules be exported to Excel, Word or PDF?

Yes. The Excel workbook has live formulas for the survey-wise LA cost, village abstract, rates, R&R, 3A / 3D schedules and checks. The gazette text exports to Word (.doc) and the schedule to Excel in the gazette layout. A PDF / HTML report, CSV and a .lac project file are also available.

Has Land Acquisition Calculator been checked?

Yes. A real ROB LA cost sheet, imported as-is, gives the same guidance-value total as the sheet, to Rs 0.00003. In the Excel export, all 348 formula cells give the same values after Excel's full recalculation. It is an estimate for checking: the competent authority determines the award.
