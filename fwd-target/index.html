FWD Target: target FWD deflection and survey planning to IRC:115

FWD Target is free Windows software that works backwards from your overlay design: it finds the target falling weight deflectometer deflection — the characteristic D0 and the deflection bowl band — that leads to that overlay under IRC:115-2014, plans the FWD survey and checks the real survey against the targets.

Features

From the overlay you plan to the FWD survey that confirms it

Enter the design overlay, existing crust, subgrade CBR and design traffic for each homogeneous section. FWD Target gives the pavement strength and deflection band behind that overlay, the test layout, and a check of the survey once it is done.

Design inputs per homogeneous section

  • Existing BC, DBM, WMM and GSB, subgrade CBR and the planned overlay (BC + DBM) for each section
  • Road category, carriageway (single, intermediate, 2-lane, 4-lane), condition Good / Fair / Bad, terrain and design msa
  • Starting moduli from IRC:37-2018 (subgrade from CBR, granular from thickness); inputs outside the IRC:115 App III.8.4 ranges are flagged

Target deflection bowl and characteristic D0

  • Layer moduli that give exactly the design overlay under IRC:115 (remaining life Eq 16 / 17, overlay in 5 mm steps), and the band that still gives it
  • The moduli the FWD should see at the survey temperature (Eq 4–5) and season (Eq 6–9)
  • Expected D0–D1200 deflection bowl band at 40 kN, SCI, remaining life and strains after the overlay
  • Overlay against characteristic D0 for the section, and a self-check through the DeflectPress engine

FWD survey plan: Table 2 spacing, staggered points

  • Test spacing from IRC:115-2014 Table 2 for the condition and lanes, staggered between LHS and RHS
  • Eq 1 minimum sample size checked for every homogeneous section
  • The list of test chainages, and an Excel field checklist with blank columns for the site record

Check the real FWD survey against the targets

  • Load KUAB .fwd files or a DeflectPress project
  • Normalised D0 against the target band per section, the characteristic D0 and the overlay it indicates
  • Run the full DeflectPress analysis of the survey at the design section breaks
  • Word report, Excel workbook and field checklist

Targets, not readings

  • Every target bowl is run back through the DeflectPress IRC:115 analysis and returns the design overlay
  • FWD Target never writes deflection readings for stations or chainages: targets are bands per section, and the field checklist leaves every reading column blank

A design aid. Targets are expectations, not measurements: the FWD survey decides the final overlay. The engineer remains responsible for the inputs and the adopted design.

FAQ

FWD target deflection questions

What does FWD Target do?

It works backwards from a strengthening design. From the planned overlay (BC + DBM), the existing crust, the subgrade CBR and the design traffic, it finds the pavement strength and the FWD deflection band that lead to that overlay under IRC:115-2014, using the same analysis as DeflectPress. It then plans the falling weight deflectometer survey and checks the real survey against the targets.

How is the target deflection worked out?

The subgrade modulus comes from the CBR (IRC:37-2018 Eq 6.2) and the granular modulus from its thickness (Eq 7.1); the bituminous modulus starts at the middle of the IRC:115 Appendix III.8.4 range for the condition. The three moduli are scaled together until the IRC:115 remaining-life and overlay analysis (Eq 16 and 17, overlay in 5 mm steps) gives exactly the design overlay. The band is every strength that still gives that overlay. The bowl (D0 to D1200 at 40 kN) is computed for the survey temperature and season.

How is the result checked?

Each target bowl is run through the DeflectPress engine: backcalculation, temperature and seasonal correction, remaining life and overlay. The overlay that comes out must equal the design overlay, and the result is shown as OK or Differs for every section.

What FWD test spacing does the survey plan use?

The IRC:115-2014 Table 2 spacing for the pavement condition and number of lanes, staggered between the two carriageway sides, with the inner lane offset on four-lane roads. Each homogeneous section gets at least the Eq 1 minimum number of points (15 for a fair pavement); the spacing is halved where a short section would fall below it.

Does FWD Target generate deflection readings?

No. Targets are bands per homogeneous section, for planning and checking only. FWD Target never writes deflection readings for stations or chainages: the field checklist lists the test chainages with every reading column left blank, and only the FWD records the deflections.

How does the survey check work?

Load the KUAB .fwd files or a DeflectPress project. For each section it shows the normalised D0, the characteristic D0 (15 % of the values higher), the points above and below the target band at the survey's own pavement temperature, and the overlay that the D0 indicates. A full DeflectPress analysis of the survey at the design section breaks gives the complete IRC:115 result.

What reports does it make?

A Word report (inputs, method, targets, bowl tables and charts, self-check, survey plan and survey check), an Excel workbook, and a separate Excel field checklist for the survey team.
